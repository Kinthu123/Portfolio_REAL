---
title: UI/UX design
summary: Research, flows, and polished screens in Figma for a web or mobile product, handed over ready for your developers to build.
order: 1
includes:
  - User research and a clear problem statement
  - User flows and information architecture
  - Wireframes and a clickable prototype
  - High fidelity screens in light and dark
  - A small design system your developers can follow
steps:
  - title: Understand
    text: We talk through the business, the people who use the product, and what is not working today. I turn that into a short brief we both agree on.
  - title: Structure
    text: I map the flows and screens, then test the structure with wireframes before any visual design starts.
  - title: Design
    text: I design the screens in Figma, build a clickable prototype, and refine it with your feedback.
  - title: Hand over
    text: You get the Figma file, the design system, and notes for every state, so building it is straightforward.
examples:
  - pos-warehouse
---

Good for a new product, or for an existing one that has grown messy. I start from the people who use it, and keep the design simple enough to build well.
